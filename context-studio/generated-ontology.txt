Below is a compact ontology for a Retail Bank Complaints Management System that connects business, IT, and regulatory concepts.

1. Entities

Business Domain

- Customer
  - Attributes:
    - customerId
    - fullName
    - dateOfBirth
    - customerSegment
    - vulnerabilityStatus
    - contactDetails

- Complaint
  - Attributes:
    - complaintId
    - complaintType
    - complaintDate
    complaintStatus
    - severity
    - channel
 - resolutionDate

- Product
  - Attributes:
    - productId
    - productName
    - productType
    - brand

- Department
  - Attributes:
    - department
    - departmentName
    - departmentHead

- Redress
  - Attributes:
    - redressId
    - redressType
    - compensationAmount
    - apologyIssued
    - redressDate

IT Domain

- SalesforceCRM
  - Attributes:
    - instanceId
    - caseReference
    - integrationStatus

- TemenosT24
  - Attributes:
    - accountId
    - transactionReference
    - coreId
    - transactionReference
    - coreBankingStatus

- ComplaintManagementSystem
  - Attributes:
    - systemId
    - workflowState
    - escalationFlag

Regulatory Domain

- FCARules
  - Attributes:
    - ruleId
    - ruleName    - complianceRequirement

- ConsumerDuty
  - Attributes:
    - dutyId
    - outcomeType
    - fairValueIndicator

- VulnerabilityPolicy
  - Attributes:
    - policyId
    - vulnerabilityCategory
    - supportRequirement

2. Relationships

Business Relationships

- Customer submits Complaint
- Complaint relatesTo Product
- Complaint handledBy Department
- Complaint resolvedBy Redress
- Customer owns Product

IT Relationships

- Complaint recordedIn SalesforceCRM
- Complaint processedIn ComplaintManagementSystem
- Customer accountManagedIn TemenosT24
- SalesforceCRM exchangesDataWith TemenosT24
- ComplaintManagementSystem integrates SalesforceCRM

Regulatory Relationships

- Complaint governedBy FCARules
- Complaint assessedAgainst ConsumerDuty
- Customer protectedBy VulnerabilityPolicy
 VulnerabilityPolicy influences Redress
- Department compliesWith FCARules

Cross-Domain Relationships

- Complaint triggers RegulatoryAssessment
- ComplaintManagementSystem stores Complaint
- SalesforceCRM tracks CustomerInteractions
- TemenosT24 provides AccountContext for Complaint

3. Sample Ontology

```ttl
@prefix ex: <http://example.com/bank-complaints#> .
@prefix rdf: <http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .

ex:Customer rdf:type owl:Class .
ex:Complaint rdf rdf:type owl:Class .
ex:Complaint rdf:type owl:Class .
ex:Product rdf:type owl:Class .
ex:Department rdf:type owl:Class .
ex:Redress rdf:type owl:Class .

ex:SalesforceCRM rdf:type owl:Class .
ex:TemenosT24 rdf:type owl:Class .
ex:ComplaintManagementSystem rdf:type owl:Class .

ex:FCARules rdf owl:Class .
ex:ConsumerDuty rdf:type owl:Class .
ex:VulnerabilityPolicy rdf:type owl:Class .

:submitsComplaint rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
   dfs:domain ex:Customer ;
    rdfs:rangedomain ex:Customer ;
    rdfs:range ex:Complaint .

ex:relatesToProduct rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain rdfs:domain ex:Complaint ;
    rdfs:range ex:Product .

ex:handledBy rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain ex:Complaint ;
    rdfs:range ex:Department .

ex:resolvedBy rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain ex:Complaint ;
    rdfs:range ex:Redress .

ex:goedBy rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain ex:Complaint ;
    rdfs:range ex:FCARules .

ex:assessedAgainst rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain ex:Complaint ;
    rdfs:range ex:ConsumerDuty .
```
