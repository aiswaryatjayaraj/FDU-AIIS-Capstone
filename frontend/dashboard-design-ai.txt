Retail Bank Complaints Management — Monitoring Workbench Design

Overview

The Monitoring Workbench is a centralized operational and governance console for AI-assisted complaints handling. It provides real-time visibility into complaint volumes, agent performance, operational risk, regulatory exposure, and human escalation workflows.

Primary Users:
- Complaints Operations Managers
- QA and Compliance Teams
- Vulnerable Specialists
- Risk and Audit Teams
- Team Leaders
- AI Operations (MLOps) Teams

Workbench Layout

Top Navigation:
- Dashboard
